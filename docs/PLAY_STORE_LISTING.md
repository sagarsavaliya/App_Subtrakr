## Full description (4000 char max)

```
SubTrakr keeps every recurring payment — personal and business — in one
place, so nothing renews without you knowing about it.

WHY SUBTRAKR

• See it all at a glance — total monthly spend, what's due this week, and
  which subscriptions are on auto-debit, in one dashboard.
• Split personal from business — track subscriptions under your own name
  and under each business you run, with GSTIN attached for filing.
• Never miss a renewal — get a reminder before every charge, with a
  one-tap "mark as paid" that updates the next due date automatically.
• Know what paid for what — attach a nickname, bank, or card network to
  each subscription (last 4 digits only — we never ask for full card
  numbers) so you can trace spend back to the right account.
• Confirm payments your way — share a payment confirmation from your bank
  app straight into SubTrakr to mark a subscription paid. SubTrakr never
  reads your SMS or notifications on its own.
• Export GST-ready reports — generate a PDF/CSV breakdown of what you
  paid, when, and the GST split, ready for filing.
• Attach receipts — keep invoices with each subscription, stored locally
  on your device.

BUILT FOR INDIA

Pricing in ₹, GST-aware exports, and support for how Indian freelancers
and small businesses actually pay for things — UPI, cards, wallets, and
bank transfers.

PRIVACY FIRST

No ad SDKs, no third-party trackers, no reading your SMS or notifications
without your say-so. Full privacy policy: https://subtrakr.me/privacy

Free to start, with room to grow as your subscription list does.
```
